The Studio

SPACE IS NEVER
JUST SPACE.

OPRELL Interiors is a Dubai-based design & build practice, established in 2020. We design, produce and deliver interiors entirely in-house — one team responsible from the first sketch to the final handover.

Our capability is end-to-end: interior design, fit-out, custom joinery, landscape and pools, and project management — delivered by a 165-strong workforce certified to ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001, operating from our own drawing office and joinery workshop.

From villas and apartments to offices, clinics and F&B spaces, every project runs on a single contract and a single programme — with the same supervisors and trades on site from day one to handover.

What sets us apart is accountability. One team owns the full scope — no handoffs, no diluted responsibility — which is why our work is documented, photographed and delivered on time, and why our clients come back.

One studio. One standard. Proven delivery.
