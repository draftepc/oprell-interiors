Who we are

The people who design a space should be the people who build it.

Established in 2020, OPRELL Interiors was built around that single idea — a Dubai design & build studio where designers, supervisors and trades are all employed by one company, working as one team on every project we take on, anywhere.

No subcontracting chain. No hand-offs. No diluted responsibility. One accountable team from the first sketch to the final handover — a drawing office and a joinery workshop under one roof, so nothing is lost between intent and execution.
