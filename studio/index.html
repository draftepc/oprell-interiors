The studio

Space is never
just space.

OPRELL Interiors & Fit-Out is a Dubai design and build practice delivering interior fit-out, villa renovation and landscape works across the UAE.

Everything is executed in-house — our own masons, carpenters, painters, electricians and supervisors. From first sketch to handover: one team, one line of responsibility.

Established in 2020 as part of the HHM group of companies, the studio was built around a simple idea: the people who design a space should be accountable for building it. No subcontracting chains, no blurred responsibility — just drawings becoming rooms under one roof.
