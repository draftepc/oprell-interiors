What we do

Everything under
one roof.

One team from first sketch to final handover — no subcontracting, no hand-offs. Six disciplines, one accountable crew, all in-house.

01

Interior Design

Concept, space planning, materials and FF&E — interiors composed around the people who use them, drawn and detailed by our in-house designers.

  • Concept & mood direction
  • Space planning & layouts
  • Materials & finishes schedule
  • FF&E selection & procurement
02

Fit-Out

Full contracting and execution — partitions, MEP, joinery and finishes by our own workforce. The drawing office and the site are the same company.

  • Partitions, ceilings & flooring
  • MEP works & commissioning
  • Custom joinery & upholstery
  • Finishes, snag & handover
03

Design + Build

One contract, one line of responsibility — the people who draw it are the people who build it. Faster programmes, no gap between intent and execution.

  • Single design & build contract
  • In-house workforce throughout
  • Fixed programme & budget
  • Post-handover support
04

Villa Renovation

Total interior renovation, extensions and upgrades for residential properties across the UAE — often while families remain in residence.

  • Full strip-out & re-fit
  • Kitchens, bathrooms & majlis
  • Extensions & structural works
  • Lighting & smart upgrades
05

Landscape & Pools

Outdoor rooms, hardscape, planting and pool works — the interior extended outside, detailed with the same discipline as the rooms inside.

  • Pool construction & refurbishment
  • Hardscape, decking & pergolas
  • Planting & irrigation
  • Outdoor lighting & kitchens
06

Project Management

Programme, procurement and site supervision — time, cost and craft under one accountable team, reported transparently every week.

  • Programme & procurement
  • Site supervision & QA/QC
  • Cost control & reporting
  • Authority approvals
